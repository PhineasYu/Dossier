# Dossier Hackathon retrospective and LinkedIn writing package

## Goal
Create one English Markdown document that Claude can use to refine a LinkedIn post about building Dossier at the Accel AI Innovate Hackathon on September 16.

The narrative will balance:
- the emotional reason for preserving childhood memories,
- the product and AI innovation behind Dossier,
- the intense hackathon journey, trade-offs, and lessons learned.

## Deliverable
Produce a standalone Markdown file containing:

1. **Project snapshot**
   - Dossier’s one-line pitch and the parent problem it solves.
   - The “one input, two outputs” concept: emotional memories plus structured family knowledge.

2. **The journey in chronological order**
   - Initial 90-second science-fair goal and five-minute presentation context.
   - Voice brain dump, child timelines, profiles, document archive, daily check-in, search, and Dossier+ chat.
   - Major visual iterations from warm note-app styling to the final tech-white system.
   - Landing, authentication, sponsor QR, demo reset, and stage fallback decisions.
   - What was deliberately cut to meet the deadline.

3. **Decision and learning record**
   - Replace private chain-of-thought with a useful, factual summary of constraints, decisions, alternatives, outcomes, and lessons.
   - Highlight fast user testing, emotional storytelling, demo reliability, and prioritisation under time pressure.

4. **Credits and event context**
   - Accel AI Innovate Hackathon, hosted by KTH AI Society.
   - Lovable, Supabase, Builderbase, Polar, ElevenLabs, other sponsors, and @Patrik Omari.
   - Use careful wording and placeholders where an exact LinkedIn handle or sponsor list still needs confirmation.

5. **Claude-ready prompt**
   - A copy-paste prompt containing the factual source material, desired tone, structure, length options, hashtag guidance, and instructions not to invent facts.

6. **LinkedIn draft**
   - One polished English post with an emotional opening, concise build story, product reveal, honest lessons, acknowledgements, and a closing reflection or invitation.
   - Include editable placeholders for tags and any final event details.
   - Add a shorter alternative opening and a compact version for easier final selection.

## Boundaries
- Do not modify the Dossier app.
- Do not expose hidden internal reasoning or fabricate personal experiences, awards, results, sponsor claims, or quotes.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed project facts from suggested phrasing and editable placeholders.
